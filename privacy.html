Privacy policy

rclone OAuth client

What this application is

This is a personal OAuth 2.0 client for rclone. It is used by one person to access their own Google Drive account. It is not offered as a service to anyone else, and no account other than the owner's is intended to use it.

What data is accessed

When the client is authorized, Google issues OAuth tokens that let rclone read and modify files in the Google Drive account the owner authorizes. The scope requested is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.

Where the data goes

Files are transferred directly between the owner's own computer and Google Drive. No copy of any file, file listing, or account data is sent to, stored on, or processed by any server operated by the owner of this site.

What is stored

OAuth tokens are stored locally by rclone in its configuration file on the machine where rclone runs. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and performs no tracking of any kind.

Third parties

No data is sold, rented, shared, or transferred to third parties. The only external service involved is Google Drive itself, which is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by deleting the rclone remote from the local rclone configuration.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
